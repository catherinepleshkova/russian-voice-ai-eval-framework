## 🎯 Executive Summary

Multilingual TTS models and conversational voice agents (e.g., ElevenLabs, OpenAI Voice Engine, Cartesia, SaluteSpeech, Yandex SpeechKit) frequently struggle with Russian-specific phonological and prosodic rules. Common failure modes include **context-dependent homograph misstressing**, **missing vowel reduction (akan'ye/ikan'ye)**, **calqued English rising intonation on yes/no questions**, and **improper palatalization in loanwords**.

This repository provides a production-ready framework designed for **AI training pipelines, RLHF audio annotation, and model benchmarking**. It translates subjective impressions of "unnatural speech" into granular, actionable linguistic parameters documented in clear English.

## 📊 1. Core Evaluation Rubric (1–5 Likert Scale)

Each audio clip is evaluated independently across three core dimensions from **1 (Critical Failure)** to **5 (Native Benchmark)**:

| Score | Pronunciation & Phonetics (P&P) | Prosody & Intonation (P&I) | Acoustic Authenticity (AA) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **5 — Flawless Native** | Native orthoepic benchmark. Accurate lexical stress, natural vowel reduction ($[o] \to [ɐ]/[ə]$), proper word-final devoicing, and organic palatalization. | Dynamic, contextually accurate communicative focus (*логическое ударение*). Authentic Russian Intonational Constructions (ИК). Natural breath and pacing. | Studio-grade signal. Organic vocal timbre, natural micro-breaths, zero metallic resonance or splicing cuts. |
| **4 — Near-Native** | 1 minor over-articulated unstressed vowel or slightly rigid consonant cluster; 100% intelligible with zero semantic ambiguity. | Appropriate cadence and sentence-type intonation; minor stiffness in clause transitions or slightly predictable rhythm. | Clean acoustic output; faint high-frequency dryness or slightly uniform breath intake. |
| **3 — Synthetic / Acceptable** | Occasional stress errors on secondary words; noticeable hyper-bookish or foreign phonetic habits, yet fully intelligible. | Flat pitch contour; distinguishes basic questions from statements, but lacks pragmatic subtext or emotional warmth. | Audible vocoder artifacts, mild robotic resonance, or noticeable micro-splicing between tokens. |
| **2 — Degraded** | Frequent misstressing, including homograph errors that alter meaning; broken palatalization (*твёрдые вместо мягких*). | Illogical pauses splitting syntactic constituents; inverted pitch contours (e.g., English-style terminal rise on Russian declaratives). | Heavy metallic distortion, unstable voice identity across the clip, or severe clipping. |
| **1 — Critical Failure** | Unintelligible phonology, hallucinated syllables, skipped tokens, or severe non-Russian phoneme mapping. | Complete prosodic collapse; monotonous syllable-timed output or erratic pitch spikes. | Corrupted audio stream, extreme static, or cut-off generation. |

## 🏷️ 2. Formal Error Taxonomy & Annotation Tags

When logging defects during QA workflows, annotators assign standardized tags, exact timestamps (`MM:SS.ms`), and structured linguistic rationales.

### Category A: Phonetics, Phonology & Lexical Stress (`PHON`)
* **`PHON-STR-LEX` (Lexical Stress Placement):** Incorrect primary stress in unambiguous words (e.g., *договОр* rendered as *\*дОговор*, *звонИт* as *\*звОнит*).
* **`PHON-STR-HOM` (Homograph Disambiguation Failure):** Model fails to use syntactic/semantic context to resolve heteronyms (e.g., *зАмок* $[ˈzamək]$ \[castle\] vs. *замОк* $[zɐˈmok]$ \[lock\]; *стОит* \[costs\] vs. *стоИт* \[stands\]).
* **`PHON-RED-VOW` (Vowel Reduction Violation):** Failure to execute standard Akan'ye/Ikan'ye reduction in unstressed syllables (e.g., pronouncing unstressed *«о»* in *молоко* as $[o]$ instead of $[ə]/[ɐ]$).
* **`PHON-CONS-PAL` (Palatalization Defect):** Incorrect rendering of soft (*мягкие*) vs. hard (*твёрдые*) consonants, especially before $[e]$ in loanwords (e.g., hyper-softening $[s^j]$ in *сервис* or failing to soften word-final $[t^j]$).
* **`PHON-CONS-ASSIM` (Regressive Assimilation & Devoicing):** Failure to devoice word-final voiced obstruents (*хлеб* must end in $[p]$, not $[b]$) or failure to merge sibilant clusters (*счёт* $\to [ɕːɵt]$).
* **`PHON-YO-REST` (Unresolved «Ё» Restoration):** Pronouncing *«е»* instead of *«ё»* when orthography omits the diaeresis (e.g., reading *все* $[fs^je]$ instead of *всё* $[fs^jo]$).

### Category B: Prosody, Intonation & Syntactic Flow (`PROS`)
* **`PROS-IK-MISMATCH` (Intonational Construction Violation):** Applying an incorrect pitch contour under Bryzgunova’s Russian Intonational System (see Section 3).
* **`PROS-RHEME-SHIFT` (Misplaced Logical Stress):** Placing the primary pitch peak ($F_0$ excursion) on auxiliary words, prepositions, or given topic (*Theme*) instead of the communicative focus (*Rheme*).
* **`PROS-PAUSE-SYN` (Syntactic Boundary Violation):** Inserting artificial pauses inside tightly bound syntactic phrases (e.g., between a preposition and its governed noun).

### Category C: Text Normalization & Register (`NORM`)
* **`NORM-NUM-CASE` (Numeral Declension Failure):** Failing to inflect digits into oblique cases dictated by syntax (e.g., reading *"более 500"* as *\*«более пятьсот»* instead of *«более пятисот»*).
* **`NORM-ABBR-EXP` (Abbreviation Expansion Error):** Misreading standard contractions or acronyms (*«и т. д.»*, *«г. Москва»*, *«МГУ»*).
* **`NORM-CODE-SW` (Code-Switching Friction):** Unnatural phonetic freezing or accent shift when reading embedded English brand names or technical terms.

## 📈 3. Reference Guide: Russian Intonational Constructions (ИК)

Because Russian has flexible word order, **intonation serves as a primary grammatical marker**. Multilingual AI models trained heavily on English data often fail to reproduce Russian Intonational Constructions (*Интонационные конструкции Е. А. Брызгуновой*):

| Contour | Communicative Function | Pitch Mechanics at Intonation Center | Typical AI / TTS Failure Mode |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ИК-1** | Neutral declarative statement | Smooth downward pitch movement on the tonic syllable. | Failing to drop pitch at the end of a sentence ("hanging" English-style cadence). |
| **ИК-2** | Wh-question (*Где? Когда?*) or direct command | Slight pitch rise + increased vocal intensity on the focus word, followed by a sharp fall. | Reading wh-questions with a terminal rising tail instead of centering energy on the interrogative pronoun. |
| **ИК-3** | General Yes/No question (no question word) | **Sharp, steep pitch rise ($F_0$ peak)** on the stressed syllable of the focus word, followed by an immediate drop on post-tonic syllables. | **Most frequent LLM error:** Applying a gradual English rise across the very end of the sentence instead of spiking on the semantic focus word. |
| **ИК-4** | Comparative incomplete question (*«А вы?»*) or official listing | Pitch dips below pre-tonic level and rises steadily through the end of the phrase. | Flat, monotone delivery that strips conversational continuity. |
| **ИК-5** | High-degree evaluative exclamation (*«Какой прекрасный день!»*) | Two prosodic centers: high rise on the first center, sustained high plateau, and fall on the second center. | Misinterpreting the exclamation as a literal wh-question (ИК-2). |

## ⚖️ 4. Edge-Case Protocols & Inter-Annotator Calibration

To maintain high Inter-Annotator Agreement (IAA), ambiguous cases follow strict decision rules:

1. **Colloquial Compression vs. Phonetic Error:**
   * *Conversational Agents:* Mild phonetic compression in high-frequency words (*«сейчас»* $\to [s^jɪˈt͡ɕas]$) is **acceptable (Score 4–5)** in informal dialogue. Hyper-slurred forms (*«щас»*, *«ваще»*) are penalized unless explicitly written in the prompt.
2. **Hard Consonant Norms in Loanwords (*Твёрдые согласные перед Е*):**
   * Words with established hard consonant norms (*бизнес, проект, сервис, темп, бэкенд, дедлайн*) must be pronounced without palatalization before *«е»*. Softening them ($[s^jɛrv^jɪs]$) is flagged under `PHON-CONS-PAL`.
3. **Broadcast Standard (Akan'ye vs. Okan'ye):**
   * All neutral prompts are benchmarked against **Contemporary Standard Russian (Literary / Broadcast Standard)**. Regional unreduced unstressed *«о»* (*оканье*) or voiced velar fricatives (*гэканье*) are flagged under `PHON-RED-VOW` unless a regional persona is specified.

## 🧪 5. Russian TTS Stress-Test Benchmark & Golden Rationales

Below is a curated diagnostic dataset of high-failure Russian prompts designed to expose structural weaknesses in TTS models, paired with gold-standard English evaluation rationales.

### 🔹 Sample #01: Contextual Homographs & Mandatory «Ё» Restoration
* **Input Prompt:** *«Наконец-то все узнали, что этот старинный замок стоит целое состояние, но двери все еще заперты на замок.»*
* **Target Linguistic Traps:**
  * *все* (everyone, $[fs^je]$) vs. *все еще* (still $\to$ *всё ещё*, $[fs^jo\ jɪˈɕːo]$).
  * *зАмок* (castle, $[ˈzamək]$) vs. *замОк* (lock, $[zɐˈmok]$).
  * *стОит* (costs, $[ˈstoɪt]$) vs. *стоИт* (stands, $[stɐˈit]$).

> **📋 Golden Evaluation Rationale (Sample #01)**  
> * **Scores:** P&P: `2/5` | P&I: `4/5` | AA: `5/5`  
> * **Defect Tags:** `PHON-STR-HOM` (`00:02.40`), `PHON-YO-REST` (`00:05.10`)  
> * **Justification:** *While the acoustic signal is clean, the model failed two critical contextual disambiguations. At `00:02.40`, «замок» was stressed on the final syllable $[zɐˈmok]$ (padlock) despite the preceding modifier «старинный» (ancient), which semantically enforces the architectural reading $[ˈzamək]$ (castle). Furthermore, at `00:05.10`, the adverbial phrase «все еще» was read literally with an unrounded front vowel $[fs^je]$ instead of restoring the mandatory «ё» $[fs^jo\ jɪˈɕːo]$ ("still").*

---

### 🔹 Sample #02: Intonational Construction ИК-3 (General Yes/No Question)
* **Input Prompt:** *«Вы уже согласовали финальный бюджет с отделом маркетинга?»*
* **Target Linguistic Traps:**
  * Identical word order to a declarative sentence; requires a sharp ИК-3 pitch peak on the focus verb (*«согласовали»*) or object (*«бюджет»*) followed by a post-tonic fall.

> **📋 Golden Evaluation Rationale (Sample #02)**  
> * **Scores:** P&P: `5/5` | P&I: `2/5` | AA: `4/5`  
> * **Defect Tags:** `PROS-IK-MISMATCH` (`00:00.80` – `00:03.90`)  
> * **Justification:** *Phonetic articulation and vowel reduction are native-like. However, the model failed to execute the Russian ИК-3 interrogative contour. Instead of producing a sharp fundamental frequency ($F_0$) rise on the communicative center («согласовали» or «бюджет»), the model applied an English-style terminal upward drift on the final word («маркетинга»). To a native Russian listener, this prosodic calque sounds unnatural and obscures the intended question focus.*

---

### 🔹 Sample #03: Complex Numeral Inflection & Sibilant Assimilation
* **Input Prompt:** *«К отчёту не хватало более восьмисот пятидесяти семи страниц с расчётами.»*
* **Target Linguistic Traps:**
  * Compound genitive numeral inflection (*восьмисот пятидесяти семи*).
  * Regressive assimilation across the cluster *«с расчётами»* (where *сч* merges into a single long soft alveolo-palatal fricative $[ɕː]$).

> **📋 Golden Evaluation Rationale (Sample #03)**  
> * **Scores:** P&P: `3/5` | P&I: `4/5` | AA: `4/5`  
> * **Defect Tags:** `PROS-PAUSE-SYN` (`00:01.90`), `PHON-CONS-ASSIM` (`00:03.60`)  
> * **Justification:** *Numeral orthography was normalized accurately in the genitive case. However, at `00:01.90`, the model inserted an unnatural 250ms micro-pause between the numeral «семи» and its governed noun «страниц», breaking syntactic cohesion. At `00:03.60`, in «расчётами», the orthographical cluster «сч» was over-articulated as two separate segments $[st͡ɕ]$ rather than coalescing into the normative long palatalized fricative $[ɕː]$.*

---

### 🔹 Sample #04: IT Loanword Hardening & Elliptical Contrast
* **Input Prompt:** *«Деплой нового микросервиса запланирован на среду, а тестирование бэкенда — на пятницу.»*
* **Target Linguistic Traps:**
  * Mandatory hard consonant before *«е»* in loanwords: *микросервиса* ($[ˈsɛrv^jɪsə]$), *тестирование* ($[tɨˈs^jt^jirəvən^jɪjə]$), *бэкенда* ($[bɨˈkɛndə]$).
  * Intonational suspension across the em-dash marking verb ellipsis.

> **📋 Golden Evaluation Rationale (Sample #04)**  
> * **Scores:** P&P: `3/5` | P&I: `3/5` | AA: `5/5`  
> * **Defect Tags:** `PHON-CONS-PAL` (`00:00.95`), `PROS-PAUSE-SYN` (`00:02.80`)  
> * **Justification:** *At `00:00.95`, the model hyper-palatalized the root-initial sibilant in «микросервиса», rendering $[s^jɛ]$ instead of the normative hard $[sɛ]$. Additionally, at `00:02.80`, the model ignored the syntactic ellipsis marked by the em-dash after «бэкенда», rushing into «на пятницу» without the obligatory pre-pause rising tone that signals an omitted predicate.*
